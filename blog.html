Planetary Boundaries PB-1 — Climate Change
"net zero"

A climate-finance benchmark released today — Senken's Pace of Change 2026, covering Europe's largest companies — puts a number on a gap the phrase itself doesn't name: actually removing the emissions that these companies' net-zero targets imply would cost roughly €51–68 billion a year, and few firms are buying removals anywhere near that scale yet.

"Net zero" permits continued emissions as long as an equivalent amount is theoretically offset elsewhere — it doesn't by itself require that the offset be permanent, independently verified, or purchased in the volume the target implies. The benchmark measures exactly that distance between the accounting term and what it would take to make the atmosphere whole.
